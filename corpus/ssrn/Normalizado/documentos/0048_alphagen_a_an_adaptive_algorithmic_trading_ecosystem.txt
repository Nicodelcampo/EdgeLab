AlphaGen 08 Regime Intelligence
# Regime Intelligence
AlphaGenâ€™s Regime Intelligence module operationalizes market regimes as statistically distinct states â€” defined by return
distribution, volatility structure, and cross-asset correlation.
Its goal is not to predict future states, but to infer the **prevailing structural environment** in which strategy species operate.
This inference guides:
- Meta-learner weight allocation
- Signal emphasis
- Risk modulation behavior
---
## Statistical State Representation
Regime inference is grounded in persistent, state-dependent features:
- **Volatility magnitude**: Realized variance, GARCH-estimated conditional variance
- **Volatility clustering**: Autocorrelation of squared returns
- **Correlation concentration**: Leading eigenvalue of the cross-asset correlation matrix
- **Liquidity conditions**: Volume-to-range ratios, bid-ask spread proxies
These features form a compact representation of the current market environment.
---
## Regime Classification Model
A probabilistic classifier maps feature vectors to discrete regime labels. Techniques include:
- Hidden Markov Models (HMMs)
- Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs)
- Hierarchical clustering
Target regimes include:
1. Low-volatility directional trend
2. Mean-reversion consolidation
3. High-volatility expansion
4. Crisis: correlation collapse and liquidity stress
The classifier outputs both:
- A discrete regime label

- A posterior probability distribution over all regimes
These posterior probabilities act as **adaptive weights** for signal relevance, exposure sizing, and strategic behavior throughout
the AlphaGen ecosystem.
