Literature Review 
Prior studies on breakout trading strategies focus on moving averages, Bollinger bands, 
and price levels. Volume anomalies are underexplored despite their predictive capacity. 
This research builds on the premise that abnormal volume frequently precedes 
directional price action (Lo & Wang, 2000; Chen et al., 2012). 
 
Market Context and Background 
In volatile markets, false breakouts often mislead traders. The goal is to improve entry 
timing by detecting legitimate breakouts triggered by sustained buying or selling 
pressure, reflected through a 4x volume surge over 5 days. 
 
Methodology 
The core trading logic evaluates each of the last five days for volume spikes 4x greater 
than the 20-day average. If such a spike occurs and the high of the day exceeds the 
previous high, a breakout signal is generated.

def avgVol = Average(volume, 20);   
def breakout =   
    (volume[1] >= 4 * avgVol[1] and high > high[1]) or   
    (volume[2] >= 4 * avgVol[2] and high > high[2]) or   
    (volume[3] >= 4 * avgVol[3] and high > high[3]) or   

    (volume[4] >= 4 * avgVol[4] and high > high[4]) or   
    (volume[5] >= 4 * avgVol[5] and high > high[5]);
