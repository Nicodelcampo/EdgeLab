## Statistical and market-data limitations

- The family band uses paired whole-session influence scores and a max-absolute Rademacher multiplier statistic across ALLsupported unique masks in this focal contract pass. With ten sessions, all1,024sign patterns are enumerated. Enumeration is not an exact finite-sample hypothesis test: independent-session blocks, symmetry and influence linearization are assumptions. It is NOT a joint correction across all six contracts or the prior research history.
- Matching uses common session/hour3/pretrigger-span40/remaining-distance10strata and minimum-count weights. Translated controls are not randomized. Width is preserved in each source/control construction, but exact width and departure-side balance within the pooled matching strata still need stricter auditing; other omitted regimes can explain a difference.
- Frozen birth dedup75 and first chronological signal per6,250-record bin precede every feature gate. Their use reduces duplicates but does not prove event independence. No future excursion, final zone lifetime or later outcome fits a feature threshold.
- quote-inferred imbalance is not exchange-native aggressor truth. Bar-density uses25ordered observed records, not necessarily original platform timestamp bars.
- Selected-session authorization is retrospective; warmup can affect calibration. Original-export provenance remains unavailable; MNQ_09-26 warmup-jump sensitivity, cross-session carry, executable fills, slippage, commissions and fresh validation are not completed here.
