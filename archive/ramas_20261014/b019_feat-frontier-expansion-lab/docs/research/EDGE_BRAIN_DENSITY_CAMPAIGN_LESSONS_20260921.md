## Brain updates

1. **Density is state, not a signal.** A low-density path or dense backstop cannot be promoted as ignition without an independently validated transition mechanism.
2. **Every directional interpretation tested failed.** Corridor traversal, wall bounce, confirmed rejection and confirmed breach all failed economically; do not alternate stories post hoc.
3. **Lifecycle fields are target leakage.** `touches`, final `state`, `ended_ms`, `end_reason` and completed timelines are forbidden in as-of fields.
4. **Gross visual behavior is not economic behavior.** The strongest geometric wall-bounce lead was barely positive at two ticks; observed-flow confirmation was already negative at two ticks; breach had near-zero gross expectancy.
5. **Cross-contract fragility is a stop signal.** Narrow positives in YM 03-26 did not replicate in YM 09-25 and YM 12-25.
6. **Cardinality assertions are governance.** Arithmetic mistakes were caught before outcomes by executable assertions and corrected before execution.
7. **Legacy HP-007 claims are downgraded.** Until independently reproduced with a new observable/data modality, they remain `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY_CLAIM`.
8. **Confirmation delay has an economic price.** Waiting for rejection/imbalance confirmation did not improve gross expectancy enough to offset later entry.
9. **Research saturation is now material.** Further YM outcome searches using only the same price/bid/ask/volume fields require a novelty gate, not another threshold grid.
