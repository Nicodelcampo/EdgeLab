# NQ prior-density representation protocol

Episode `EPISODE-NQ-BT2A-PRIOR-DENSITY-009` recursively improves the field representation after Episode 008 showed saturation when the current zone is included.

On the identical seven deterministic NQ contract-month samples, measure same-direction support density and confluence twice: once using only zones available strictly before the current zone, and once including the current zone. Record both and the density delta. No target, return, entry, stop, validation or holdout field is permitted.

The result will determine whether pre-existing density has usable dynamic range and whether future campaigns should represent a wall as prior structure, current formation strength, or both separately.

**Aporte al referente:** makes the Brain alter its own measurement vocabulary in response to target-free evidence rather than tuning outcome thresholds.
