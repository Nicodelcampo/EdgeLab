# YM/BT2A density-corridor discovery protocol

Campaign: `CAMP-YM-BT2A-CORRIDOR-003`

Test whether a BT2A event becomes economically useful with a formation-known supporting density wall behind it, a low-density path ahead, directional ignition and a terminal opposition wall. This is a new conditioned family, not a post-hoc extension of the rejected generic retest grid.

Only formation-known geometry, direction, volume/rows/fraction and score/threshold may contribute. Final touches, final state, invalidation time, end reason and timelines are forbidden. The field is evaluated as-of the signal tick. Entry is the first later observed tick in the same session or abstention.

Stage 1 contains 2,304 entry contexts. It varies field mode, sigma, void threshold, terminal-wall threshold, backstop threshold, corridor width, causal pre-signal momentum and formation strength. Exit is fixed: terminal wall minus one tick, six-tick stop, 1,000-tick maximum hold, break-even off.

A survivor needs at least 100 trades, positive expectancy after six all-in ticks, Holm-adjusted one-sided p below 0.05 at four ticks, and positive four-tick expectancy in at least two discovery contracts. At most 12 diverse entries may enter the 54-spec Stage-2 exit grid. Validation cannot be inspected until candidates are serialized and hashed. H2-2026 remains sealed.
