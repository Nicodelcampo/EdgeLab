Prohibited Activities (modified 2026-07-31). You may not share Accounts. Pay fees only with bank account or card in your own name; Rewards only to a bank account in your own name. No chargeback in bad faith. No false documents. You may not use VPNs, proxy servers, cloud servers, anonymizing tools to conceal identity, device or location to evade rules or geographic restrictions. You may not use the Company Services abusively or to exploit or game the purpose or spirit of the Program.
Trading Without Stop Losses or Risk Management: All trades must have either pending or mental stop losses and a well-defined risk management strategy. Trading without these measures is strictly prohibited.
High-Risk Strategies: Strategies that involve small profit targets while risking disproportionately large amounts are not allowed. For example, setting a five-tick profit target with a 150-tick stop loss demonstrates unacceptable risk management.
Using the Trailing Threshold as a Stop Loss: Traders are prohibited from using the account's full threshold as a stop-loss mechanism to absorb large losses, leading to account liquidation.
Stockpiling Evaluation Accounts: Purchasing multiple discounted evaluation accounts to cycle through and intentionally blow up accounts in pursuit of windfall profits is not permitted.
Unsustainable Strategies: Any trading that fails to demonstrate consistent growth and sustainability is prohibited.
Deviating from Professional Standards: Traders must employ strategies and risk management consistent with those they would use in a personally funded account at a registered broker.
Manipulation of the simulated trading environment: prohibited, including High Frequency Trading (HFT) or any other exploitative strategies, trying to manipulate the system for erroneous fills or breaching the maximum allowed contracts. This includes non-directional bracket trading, where orders are left open on both sides of the market.
Account and Resource Sharing: Sharing MAC addresses, computers, IPs, credit cards, or trade copying with other traders is strictly forbidden. Violations result in account closure and forfeiture of funds.
Multiple Account Creation: Creating multiple user accounts is prohibited and is a bannable offense.
Holding open trade positions through the market close is prohibited. All open trade positions must be closed prior to the market close. Traders will forfeit their accounts and all associated balances.
No Automation or Algorithm Usage allowed: Rewards are intended to recognize human traders, not automated systems executing preprogrammed logic.
NEWS TRADING: Trading during news is allowed for your normal trading strategy. News trading strategies that chase the market or place orders on both sides to gamble the outcome of news is not allowed.
No Hedging of Any Kind. Directional Trading only. Holding both long and short positions simultaneously on the same or correlated instrument is strictly prohibited.
Failure to adhere may result in placement in Trader Probation.
