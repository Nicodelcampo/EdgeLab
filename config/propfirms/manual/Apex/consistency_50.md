50% Consistency Requirement. No single trading day accounts for more than 50% of your total accumulated profit at the time of a payout request. Applies to profits earned since your last approved payout, or since account inception. Your largest profitable trading day must represent less than 50% of your net profit since your last payout request. If not met, the payout request option will not be available; the account remains active. Reset after payout: the consistency calculation resets. Highest Profit Day / 0.5 = Minimum net profit required since last payout. Being above 50% consistency does not fail your account. Losing days reduce net profit and affect consistency. There is no time limit to satisfy the consistency percentage. If no profit or at a loss since last payout, consistency cannot be calculated and you won't qualify.
