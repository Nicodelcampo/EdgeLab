Intraday Trailing Drawdown. Threshold follows the Peak Balance including realized and unrealized gains, in real time; never moves down. If breached, positions liquidate; Evaluation fails or PA closes. Max intraday drawdown 25K $1,000, 50K $2,000, 100K $3,000, 150K $4,000. PA: trailing stops at Starting Balance + $100. Evaluations Rithmic and Wealthcharts: threshold stops trailing at the Target Profit balance. Evaluations Tradovate: trails indefinitely with the peak balance. Does not reset daily.
