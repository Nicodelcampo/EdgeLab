EOD Drawdown Explained. Calculated once daily at market close. Enforced in real time during the next trading session. If reached, positions close automatically and account is failed (evaluation) or closed (PA). Threshold never moves downward. The EOD Threshold is recalculated once per trading day at 4:59:59 PM ET, based on the account's closing balance. It always trails the highest achieved EOD balance and never moves downward.
When EOD Drawdown Stops Trailing. Performance Accounts: Once the EOD Threshold reaches Starting Balance + $100, it stops increasing. Example for a 50K EOD PA: Threshold Stop Level = $50,100, when the Highest EOD Balance reaches $52,100. Evaluations Rithmic and Wealthcharts: EOD Threshold stops trailing and becomes fixed when it reaches an amount equal to the Target Profit balance. Example 50K: Threshold Stop Level = $53,000 when the Highest EOD Balance closes above $55,000. Evaluations Tradovate: EOD Drawdown trails indefinitely with the peak EOD account balance.
The DLL limits how much an account can lose within a single trading day; if it is reached, all open positions are automatically liquidated and trading is paused for the remainder of the day, but the account remains active. The EOD Threshold, if touched at any time, results in immediate liquidation and failure of both Evaluations and PAs. The account may experience intraday drawdown, but it may never touch or cross the EOD Threshold level at any time.
