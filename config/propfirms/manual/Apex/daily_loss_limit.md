Daily Loss Limit Explained. Fixed for the entire session, does not trail intraday. If it is reached, positions close automatically and trading pauses for the day; account remains active. DLL resets daily at 6PM ET. DLL applies to total account equity including realized and unrealized losses. Liquidation at market price; final balance may be slightly above or below the DLL threshold. EOD Evaluations daily loss limit: 25K $500, 50K $1,000, 100K $1,500, 150K $2,000; does not increase with profits. There is no DLL for Intraday Drawdown Evaluations. In PA the DLL scales by tier (see scaling). DLL can decrease if balance declines but never below Level 1. Hitting the DLL does not change the EOD Threshold.
