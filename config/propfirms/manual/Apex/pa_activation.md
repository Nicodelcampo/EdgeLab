PA Activation. Evaluation reviewed at market close 4:59:59 PM ET, marked Passed after 6 PM ET. 7 calendar days from Passed to pay the PA Activation Fee; cannot be extended; if missed the opportunity expires and a new Evaluation must be passed. Activation fee is one-time, not refundable, not transferable. If a PA is disqualified, liquidated, or closed for inactivity it is permanently closed; a new Evaluation and a new activation fee are required. A passed Evaluation is locked to prevent additional trading.
