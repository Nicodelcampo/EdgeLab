EOD Payouts. Up to weekly payouts with 100% payout split. Minimum 5 trading days that meet the required minimum daily profit. Minimum payout amount: $500. 50% consistency rule applies. Maximum 6 payouts per Performance Account.
EOD Performance Account Payouts: Account Size / Min Trade Days / Min Daily Profit / Safety Net / Min Balance to Request / Max Payouts. 25K / 5 / $100 / $26,100 / $26,600 / 6. 50K / 5 / $250 / $52,100 / $52,600 / 6. 100K / 5 / $300 / $103,100 / $103,600 / 6. 150K / 5 / $350 / $154,100 / $154,600 / 6.
Each account must complete at least 5 trading days before a payout can be requested. A qualifying trading day must meet the required minimum daily profit. For example, on a 50K EOD PA, you need to have a minimum of 5 trading days with a minimum of $250 profit for each of the days. Only days that meet the minimum daily profit count toward the 5-day requirement.
50% Consistency Requirement: No single profitable trading day may account for 50% or more of total profit earned since your last approved payout. If this requirement is not satisfied, the payout request option will not be available until you reach 50% consistency.
Safety Net Requirement: The Safety Net is calculated as your account's drawdown limit plus $100. This threshold must be maintained for the lifetime of the Performance Account. Only profit above the safety net is eligible to be requested for a payout.
The minimum payout amount is $500 per request, regardless of account size.
Each Performance Account may receive a maximum of six payouts. After 6 payouts, the PA is closed.

You should trade as if the requested payout amount has already been removed from your balance. If your balance falls below the required threshold after submitting a request, the payout will be denied automatically.
