EOD Performance Accounts (PA). No intraday trailing drawdown. EOD Drawdown calculated once per day at market close and enforced intraday. Daily Loss Limit (DLL) enforced intraday. Tier Based Scaling. 100% Payout Split (upon meeting payout eligibility requirements). The EOD Performance Account is a Simulated Funded (Sim Funded) account awarded after you pass the EOD Evaluation.
Account Parameters: 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K. Max Drawdown (EOD) $1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,000. Scaling Tier Based Yes. Max Contracts 2 / 4 / 6 / 10. DLL Tier Based Yes. Inactivity Rule Yes.
Your account balance, including unrealized PnL, may never touch or fall below the End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown threshold. If it does, all positions are automatically liquidated and the PA is permanently closed. Engaging in prohibited activities, including hedging violations or any form of rule circumvention, will result in immediate account closure.
You may hold up to 20 PAs active at the same time. This cap applies across all PA accounts combined, whether different size, EOD, or Intraday Trailing Drawdown. The Performance Account is a Simulated Funded (Sim Funded) account. Hitting the DLL pauses trading for the remainder of the session. Position size increases as you move up Scaling Levels based on your End-of-Day balance, up to the account's maximum level. Approved payouts are issued at a 100% payout split once payout eligibility requirements are met.
