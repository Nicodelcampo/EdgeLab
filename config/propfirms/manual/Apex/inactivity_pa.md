Inactivity Policy on PA (modified 2026-09-11). To remain active you must record at least 2 trading days with $50 or more in net profit within every rolling 30-day period (calendar days). After 15 days of inactivity the account moves to dormant. If 30 consecutive calendar days pass without meeting this threshold the account will be closed due to inactivity; accumulated rewards and payout eligibility are forfeited; cannot be reinstated. Trading alone does not prevent closure. A breakeven day does not count.
