Apex Live Prop Trading Program FAQ (modified 2026-09-24). Invitation-based; Apex may move a trader to live at any time based on trading style, P&L, account count, speed of simulated profit accumulation. Monitoring may begin after three consecutive withdrawals from one single PA. Once selected, all Evaluation Accounts are closed and Performance Accounts deactivated; simulated profits go to a Bonus Vault that is not real money. Declining the invitation: account fully deactivated, final reward payment of $3,000 total, Bonus Vault forfeited.
Live account: $0 starting balance, $3,000 end-of-day drawdown, 3 mini / 30 micro contracts, no DLL at Level 1. Safety net: once profit reaches $3,100 the drawdown locks at +$100; payouts only from profits above $3,100. Profit split 90% to the trader. Daily payout requests, minimum $500, no cap. Level 2 ($10,000-$50,000): 10 mini, $5,000 DLL. Up to 5 live accounts, each added after $4,500 profit, max based on PA count at graduation. Bonus Vault: 20% of monthly live withdrawals (40% first month), paid the 15th of the following month, only if account active through month end. Inactivity: no trades for 30 days may close the account. Professional data about $150/month covered by Apex; commissions set by the broker. All trades must be closed by 4:50 PM ET. No hedging, no group trading, avoid limit-up and limit-down. Automation and algorithm usage are not allowed. Requalification Eval-to-Live: $199, minimum 5 days, 40% consistency, $250 minimum daily profit, $4,000 target, $2,000 intraday trailing drawdown, $1,000 flatten loss limit, 3 mini.
