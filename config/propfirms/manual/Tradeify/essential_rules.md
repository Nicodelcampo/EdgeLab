Essential Trading Rules (2026-08-26). Growth Evaluation daily loss limit $1,250 for $50k, $2,500 for $100k, $3,750 for $150k. Select Evaluation no daily loss limit. Funded: Select Daily DLL $1,000 50k; Growth and Lightning DLL $1,250 50k, $2,500 100k, $3,750 150k; 25k Lightning has no DLL. End-of-Day trailing drawdown on all accounts, enforced in real time against net liquidation including unrealized P&L. Growth $2,000 drawdown locks floor at $50,100 once EOD balance reaches $52,100.
All positions must be closed before market close; no holding positions overnight. Close all positions by 4:45 PM Eastern Time; holiday early close 12:59 PM ET. Positions left open are auto-closed without failure. Trading day 6:00 PM to 5:00 PM ET.
Max contracts: 25k 1 mini / 10 micro; 50k 4 mini; 100k 8 mini; 150k 12 mini. 10 micro = 1 mini.
Prohibited: Hedging, including opposing positions on correlated products and positions held across multiple accounts. Exploiting platform errors. High-frequency trading bots not allowed.
Algorithmic Trading allowed if you own the strategy exclusively, not shared with other traders or firms, not high-frequency trading, can prove ownership.
Microscalping: over 50% of your trades AND profits must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds; funded accounts only; otherwise you won't be able to request a payout.
Payout days: Select Daily none; Select Flex 5 winning days; Growth 5 profitable days; Lightning none. Minimum activity: at least 1 trade per week, evaluation and funded.
News trading allowed at your own risk with restrictions around major economic events.
