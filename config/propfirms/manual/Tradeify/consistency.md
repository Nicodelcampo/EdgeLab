Consistency Rule (2026-08-19). No single day's profit (commissions not included) may exceed a set percentage of total profits to request a payout; at or below the limit passes. Growth Sim Funded 35%. Select Evaluation 40% during evaluation only. Lightning Funded (after Sep 12 2025): first payout 20%, second 25%, third and subsequent 30%. Biggest End of Day PnL / Consistency % = Total needed. After an approved payout the consistency resets. Losing days reduce total profit. Does not restrict trading; only payout eligibility. Other payout conditions still apply: profit goal / minimum balance, minimum profitable trading days (5 for Growth), the 10-second rule, and the maximum payout cap for that payout number.
