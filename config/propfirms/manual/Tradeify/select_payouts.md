Select Flex and Select Daily payout policies. You must have positive net profit during each payout cycle to request a payout (loss recovery required). Only one payout request open at a time.
Select Flex: max drawdown 25K $1,000, 50K $2,000, 100K $3,000, 150K $4,500 EOD; no Daily Loss Limit; no consistency. Payout every 5 winning days. Winning day minimum profit 25K $100, 50K $150, 100K $200, 150K $250, 300K $750. Payout up to 50% of total profits (balance minus starting balance), capped per payout (accounts after Sep 1 2026): 25K $1,250, 50K $2,500, 100K $3,500, 150K $4,500 (before Sep 1 2026: $1,250 / $3,000 / $4,000 / $5,000). No minimum balance. Minimum payout $250. Drawdown locks at start+$100 at EOD balance start+drawdown+$100 or immediately when requesting a payout.
Select Daily: max drawdown 25K $1,000, 50K $2,000, 100K $2,500, 150K $3,500; Daily Loss Limit $500 / $1,000 / $1,250 / $1,750. Buffer $1,100 / $2,100 / $2,600 / $3,600, cannot withdraw below buffer. Daily Continuity Rule: request up to 2x the profit earned since last payout, capped (after Sep 1 2026) $600 / $1,250 / $1,750 / $2,500. Minimum payout $250.
Funded contract scaling by EOD equity: 50K starts 2 mini, 3 mini at $1,500, 4 mini at $2,000. 25K 1 mini then 2 at $1,500. 100K 3/4/5/8 at 0/$1,500/$2,000/$3,000. 150K 3/4/5/8/12 at 0/$1,500/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500. Scaling triggers cumulative.
Path to Live: considered after 3 payouts on a single account or 10 total payouts; not automatic. Maximum 5 funded accounts.
