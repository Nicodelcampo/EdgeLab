# Dense-wall first-retest result

Decision: `STOP_NO_SURVIVOR`.

The preregistered campaign evaluated 1,296 causal wall-retest contexts on 8,056,713 discovery ticks. There were 2,293 executable first-retest events; 466 policies reached 100 non-overlapping trades.

No policy survived. The best observed context had 123 trades and +2.211 gross ticks: +0.211 after two ticks of cost, but −1.789 after four and −3.789 after six. It was positive in only one of three discovery contracts and Holm p=1.

This rejects the claim that BT2A density/confluence, departure and first retest are sufficient for an economically robust YM bounce. The small two-tick hint is retained only as a regime lead, not evidence.

Validation and H2-2026 remained unopened.
