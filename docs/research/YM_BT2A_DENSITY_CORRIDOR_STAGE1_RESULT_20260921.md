# YM/BT2A density-corridor Stage 1 result

Decision: `STOP_NO_SURVIVOR`.

The causal corridor campaign evaluated 2,304 preregistered entry contexts on 8,056,713 discovery ticks and 2,645 BT2A zones. Of those zones, 1,718 had a causal opposition wall 4–30 ticks ahead. Seven hundred four policies reached 100 non-overlapping trades.

No policy was positive after six all-in ticks, Holm-significant at four ticks and positive in at least two contracts. Best observed gross expectancy was +1.411 ticks over 190 trades, equivalent to −0.589/−2.589/−4.589 ticks after 2/4/6 ticks of cost. It was negative in every contract and had Holm p=1.

The result rejects this precise family: a BT2A signal plus low forward density, supporting backstop, terminal wall, momentum and formation strength is not sufficient ignition. It does not reject density fields as state variables. The next distinct mechanism is collision/rejection at a dense wall after departure, evaluated as first retest with tick-level ordering.

Validation and H2-2026 remained unopened.

**Aporte al referente:** records a fully causal negative result and narrows the mechanism from generic corridor traversal to separately testable wall-collision dynamics.
