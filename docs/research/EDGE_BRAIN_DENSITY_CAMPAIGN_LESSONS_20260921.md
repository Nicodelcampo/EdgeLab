# Edge Brain lessons — causal density and void corridors

## Episode outcome

Four preregistered causal families were run after the generic BT2A economic search failed:

| Family | Policies | Eligible | Survivors | Best net 4 ticks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BT2A-direction corridor traversal | 2,304 | 704 | 0 | −2.589 |
| Dense-wall departure + first retest | 1,296 | 466 | 0 | −1.789 |
| Independent momentum ignition + corridor | 10,368 | 939 | 0 | −2.822 |
| Observed wall-contact rejection + aggressor flow | 3,456 | 455 | 0 | −2.195 |

All used canonical YM ticks, next-observed-tick fills, same-session paths, non-overlapping trades, explicit 2/4/6 all-in costs and multiplicity correction. The fourth family additionally required an actual post-contact transition before entry and preregistered a session-cluster bootstrap gate. It produced no pre-cluster survivor. Validation and H2-2026 were not opened.

## Brain updates

1. **Density is state, not a signal.** A low-density path or dense backstop cannot be promoted as ignition without an independently validated transition mechanism.
2. **Separate mechanisms.** Corridor traversal, wall rejection, retest bounce and aggressive breach are distinct hypotheses and must never rescue each other post hoc.
3. **Lifecycle fields are target leakage.** `touches`, final `state`, `ended_ms`, `end_reason` and completed timelines are forbidden in as-of density fields.
4. **Gross visual behavior is not economic behavior.** The strongest geometric wall-bounce lead was barely positive at two ticks; the stronger observed-flow confirmation was already negative at two ticks.
5. **Cross-contract fragility is a stop signal.** Narrow positives in YM 03-26 did not replicate in YM 09-25 and YM 12-25.
6. **Cardinality assertions are governance.** Two arithmetic mistakes were caught before outcomes by executable assertions and corrected before preregistration/execution.
7. **Legacy HP-007 claims are downgraded.** Until reproduced with causal formation fields, tick ordering, non-overlap and costs, they remain `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY_CLAIM`.
8. **Confirmation delay has an economic price.** Waiting for rejection/imbalance confirmation did not improve gross expectancy enough to offset later entry.
