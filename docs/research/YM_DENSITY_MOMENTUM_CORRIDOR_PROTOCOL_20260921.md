# Momentum-ignited causal corridor protocol

Campaign `CAMP-YM-DENSITY-MOMENTUM-005` separates the density transport medium from the BT2A direction. BT2A formation supplies a causal event clock and updates the field; trade direction comes only from pre-signal momentum. The hypothesis is that a void is not a signal by itself but may reduce friction after independent ignition.

The frozen 20,736-policy family crosses total/directional density, three Gaussian scales, void/wall/backstop thresholds, four corridor widths, three momentum lookbacks and thresholds, an all-hours versus US-hours gate, and all/low/high prior-100-tick range gates. Management is fixed: structural terminal wall minus one tick, SL 6, 1,000-tick maximum hold and no break-even. Costs are 2/4/6 ticks.

Entry occurs strictly after the event tick. Trades are non-overlapping per policy and contract. Survival requires at least 100 trades, positive six-tick expectancy, Holm p<0.05 at four ticks and positive four-tick expectancy in at least two contracts. No validation or H2-2026 outcomes may be opened before a candidate lock exists.

**Aporte al referente:** tests whether momentum supplies the missing ignition identified by the failed BT2A-direction corridor campaign while accounting explicitly for session and volatility regime.
