# Aggressive dense-wall breach Stage 1 result

Decision: `STOP_NO_SURVIVOR`.

The preregistered continuation campaign evaluated 2,304 contexts over 8,056,713 discovery ticks. There were 2,091 zones with executable first retests; 308 policies reached 100 non-overlapping trades.

No policy reached the pre-cluster survival gate. The best observed context produced 103 trades and only +0.126 gross ticks, equivalent to −1.874/−3.874/−5.874 ticks after 2/4/6 ticks of cost. It was negative at four ticks in every contract and Holm p=1.

This rejects the tested continuation interpretation even more strongly than the bounce interpretation. Dense BT2A walls were not economically useful as support/resistance, rejection structures, void boundaries or aggressive failure structures under the available YM observables.

Validation and H2-2026 remained unopened. Outcome-guided widening of this family is prohibited.

**Aporte al referente:** closes the last preregistered directional interpretation of the current density-wall representation and prevents oscillating post hoc between bounce and breakout stories.
